Support

Something broken, or something wrong? Email support@hermi.world. It goes to both of us.

In an emergency, call 911. Hermi is not an emergency service and nobody monitors it for emergencies.

Report a post or a person

You can report any post or profile from inside the app. Open the post or the person’s profile and choose Report. A reported post disappears for you right away.

  • A real person reads every report. We act within 24 hours.
  • We remove content that breaks the rules and ban people who abuse others. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
  • Can’t use the app, or need to add details? Email us with the username and what happened. Screenshots help.

Block someone

Open their profile in the app and choose Block. Blocking works in both directions: you stop seeing each other’s posts and profile. You can unblock later in the same place.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account and personal data, including photos, location history and check-ins, and disconnects Hermi from your Sign in with Apple. Read more under Deleting your account in the privacy policy.

If you cannot open the app, email us with your username and we will help. Deleting the app from your iPhone does not delete your Hermi account.

Questions people ask

Who can use Hermi?
People who are 18 or older, in the United States, with an iPhone running iOS 17 or later. You sign in with Apple and verify a Columbia or Barnard email address.
Why do you want my school email?
Only to check that you are a student. We email you a code, then keep just a scrambled (hashed) fingerprint of the address so one school email cannot verify many accounts. We do not keep the address itself.
Does Hermi track me all the time?
No. Hermi records a trail of your precise location only while an outing is running, and deletes those location points automatically after 90 days. It also notes where you are at the moment you check in. See the privacy policy.
Can my friends see where I am right now?
No live location. Friends can see the places you checked in, and checking in is a choice you make at each stop. Turn on Ghost mode in the app to hide your check-ins from the friends list, profiles and the social map. Posts you choose to share stay visible.
How do I add a friend?
In person. You tap phones or scan each other’s code. There is no friend search and no friend request.
My check-in was refused. Why?
A check-in has to be earned at the place. Tap the venue’s tag while you are there, or stay at the stop for a few minutes with a good GPS signal (outdoors helps).
What does the AI see?
Hermi uses Google’s Gemini for the planning assistant and to check posts before they go live. We do not send it your name, username, Apple ID or school email. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent in AI features.
Is Hermi affiliated with Columbia?
No. Hermi is an independent project, and Columbia and Barnard are not involved with it.
Something is broken.
Email us with what you were doing, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can.
