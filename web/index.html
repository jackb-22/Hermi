Come out of your shell.

Hermi turns “we should go out sometime” into an outing that actually happens, and proves it did.

Join the waitlist

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. For people 18 and older in the United States, built for Columbia and Barnard students.

How it works

  1. Discover real places. Browse a pixel map of New York, ranked by where people have actually been.
  2. Plan with friends, and AI. Put stops in order with times. An AI button suggests changes, and you accept or ignore each one.
  3. Go, and check in in person. Tap the venue’s tag or let GPS confirm you stayed a few minutes. Photos can only be taken in the app, at the place.
  4. Come home to a recap. See your route, your XP and a post your friends can trust, because it is stamped as verified in person.

Social, only in person

  • Prove you went. Check-ins and photos are checked against the place and the time.
  • No friend search. You add friends by tapping phones. Streaks count real hangouts.
  • Nothing to like. No likes, no comments. The feed is finite and ends with “go outside”.
