1. The short version

  • Hermi helps you plan outings and prove you went. To do that it uses your precise location during outings, your check-ins, photos and posts, and your plans.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Hermi uses Google’s Gemini AI to help plan outings and to check posts before they go live. See what is sent.
  • Raw location points are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • You can delete your account in the app. That deletes your account and personal data, including photos, location history and check-ins.
  • Hermi is for people 18 or older, in the United States.
  • Hermi is not affiliated with Columbia University or Barnard College.

Text Hermi (iMessage), only if you link a phone

Some versions of Hermi let you link a phone number or Apple Messages address and text Hermi a plan. If you link one, we store it on your account until you unlink it or delete your account. Messages you send Hermi are read to build the plan, and are sent to Gemini together with the sender’s phone number or address as it appears in the chat. If Hermi is added to a group chat, it keeps the chat’s recent lines for about two days so it can read them when someone asks it to plan, and it posts check-in notes (your name or username and the place) into the chat of a plan that is linked to it. If you never link a phone, we collect nothing from this feature about you.

Collected automatically

  • Technical logs. Our servers log each request: the IP address, the time, the address requested (which can include the map area or coordinates the app was showing, for example when you open the Feed) and any error. We use logs to keep Hermi running and secure.
  • Abuse counters. To stop abuse we count requests by account and by IP address for up to about an hour, for example how many school-email codes one account can ask for.
  • Crash reports from Apple. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may show us crash reports. We do not add our own analytics or advertising tools to the app.

We do not collect your contacts, your advertising identifier, or anything from your photo library other than a profile photo you choose.

4. How we use it

We use your information only to run and protect Hermi:

  • to sign you in, verify you are a student and show your profile;
  • to confirm check-ins, award XP, build recaps, streaks and your Score;
  • to show you places, plans and posts, and to power the AI features;
  • to deliver notifications and the emails you ask for;
  • to review posts and reports, remove content, and suspend or ban accounts that break the rules;
  • to find and fix problems, and to keep Hermi secure and prevent abuse;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

6. AI features

Hermi uses Google’s Gemini models, through Google’s paid Gemini API, in these places. When a feature runs, Hermi sends Google the information listed. Our code decides what is allowed, and the AI only proposes.

  • The planning assistant (the AI button). What you type or the suggestion you tap; earlier turns of that conversation; the time in New York; your plan’s travel mode, start time and every stop with its name, category, tags and times; details about places on your plan, such as opening hours and our review summary; and the short notes Hermi saved about your preferences.
  • Questions about places. When you ask about a place, Gemini can use Grounding with Google Maps. We send your question and the coordinates of one of your plan’s stops (or a point in Manhattan if the plan is empty). Hermi shows the Google Maps sources it used.
  • Next-stop suggestions. A short list of nearby candidate places (name, category, tags and walking time), when you would leave, sunset time, a one-line weather summary, and the stops of your plan so far.
  • Stay lengths and plan names. Stop names, categories and arrival times.
  • Review summaries. A place’s name and the text of its recent reviews, each shortened, with their “would go again” answers. No names or usernames.
  • Safety checks on posts. Before a post goes live, its caption or review text and up to three images (your photos, or frames from a clip) are sent to Gemini to decide whether the post is allowed. A post is checked again if someone reports it.
  • Text Hermi. If you use it, the messages Hermi reads to find a plan, with the sender’s phone number or address.

What is not sent: your name, username, school email, Apple identifier, or profile photo. But whatever you type to the AI, or put in a caption, review or photo, is sent, so please keep sensitive details out of them.

How Google handles it. We use the paid tier of the Gemini API. Under Google’s terms for paid services, Google does not use our prompts or the responses to improve its products. It logs them for a limited time, only to detect and prevent misuse of its service, and may store or cache them in any country where it has facilities. We do not use your information to train AI models.

You stay in control. The assistant shows its changes as suggestions, and nothing changes your plan until you accept it. It can be wrong, so check opening hours and addresses. Safety checks are automatic, and a person reads every report and reviews posts the automatic check could not decide.

Consent. Hermi asks you to agree to this before you use it. If you do not agree to your information going to Google’s Gemini API as described, do not use Hermi’s AI features. Because every post is checked this way before it goes live, you could not post either.

8. Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and personal data, including photos, location history and check-ins. We also tell Apple to disconnect Hermi from your Sign in with Apple.

  • Your plan share links stop working.
  • Numbers that do not identify you, such as how many people have been to a place, may not change.
  • Copies in backups age out on their schedule, as described above.
  • We may keep a limited record where the law requires it, or where we need it to handle a safety report.

If you cannot open the app, email support@hermi.world.

9. Your choices

  • Location. You choose in iOS Settings whether Hermi may use your location, and whether it may keep doing so in the background. Without it, check-ins do not work, because Hermi has to confirm you are at the place. You can still browse and plan.
  • Ghost mode and Open to plans. Turn either on or off in the app.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Text Hermi. Unlink your phone in the app to stop it acting on your messages.
  • Report and block. You can report any post or person and block anyone, from the app.
  • Your data. Email us to ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct it. We will reply within 30 days.

10. Security, age, location and changes

Security. The Hermi app and our servers talk over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We keep your school email only as a hash. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that nothing will ever go wrong.

Age. Hermi is for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

United States only. Hermi is offered in the United States. Our service providers may handle information in the United States and in other countries.

Changes. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when the current version started.
