Acknowledgements

Hermi is built on other people’s work. Here is who we thank, and what we are required to say.

Map and place data

  • Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). See openstreetmap.org/copyright.
  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles. Data from OpenStreetMap.
  • Place information: places such as names, categories and addresses come from Overture Maps Foundation data. Overture assembles its Places data from several contributors, including Meta, Microsoft and Foursquare, each under its own licence (CDLA Permissive 2.0, Apache 2.0 or CC0 1.0). The Foursquare data was transformed to the Overture schema. See Overture’s attribution page for the full list.
  • Borough outlines used for your Adventures progress are derived from Overture Maps division and water data, which includes © OpenStreetMap contributors data under the ODbL.
  • Shoreline mask: the offline mask that keeps pins on land is built from data the City of New York publishes on NYC Open Data: the DCP Borough Boundaries and the OTI NYC Planimetric Database (Hydrography).
  • Grounding with Google Maps. Some answers about places use Google Maps. Where Hermi shows them, it shows the Google Maps sources. Google Maps is a trademark of Google LLC.

Weather

Weather forecasts are provided by Apple Weather. See Apple Weather data sources for the sources behind them. Apple Weather and Weather are trademarks of Apple Inc.

If Apple’s forecast is unavailable, Hermi can use Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
